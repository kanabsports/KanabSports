Founding partnership · Year one

One hometown partner.
All year.

Honey's Marketplace becomes the exclusive founding sponsor across Kanab Sports for the first 12 months - visible where local families check scores, schedules, registration, teams and community sports.

Founding partner rate $500 per month · 12-month founding term
The big idea

Honey's owns the sponsorship lane.

For year one, Kanab Sports will not sell another recurring site, portal or category sponsorship. The only exception is individual KHS win celebrations, which may carry their own event sponsor. That keeps the everyday Kanab Sports experience clean - and makes Honey's the name families repeatedly see beside it.

1

First-year exclusivity

Honey's is the only general sponsor across Kanab Sports during the initial 12-month term. Existing demo/house sponsor treatments are removed when the partnership goes live. Unpaid community acknowledgments and operating vendors are not treated as sponsors.

Included

A simple package with visible value.

01

Presenting placement

Prominent "Presented by Honey's Marketplace" recognition on the Kanab Sports homepage, linked directly to Honey's.

02

Sitewide founding-partner credit

Consistent, tasteful recognition across key Kanab Sports experiences without turning the site into an ad wall.

03

Social media included

Minimum two dedicated Honey's sponsor posts each month, plus natural sponsor mentions around major Kanab Sports moments. Kanab Sports handles the copy, design and posting.

04

Bulk tee perk

Discounted bulk pricing on Kanab Sports tees when Honey's orders 15 or more at a time. Final price follows the selected garment and print cost.

05

Clickable traffic

Sponsor placements can link to Honey's homepage, weekly ad, digital coupons or another approved destination.

06

Quarterly recap

Simple sponsor reporting using the audience and click data Kanab Sports has available after launch - without inflated reach or sales promises.
