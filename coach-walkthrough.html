A coach’s first game on Pep

Illustrated with the actual portal screens and a fictional travel basketball team.

1

Open Coach Portal

On the Kanab Sports homepage, choose Coach Portal in the top navigation. Sign-in opens in a separate window or tab.

2

Sign in

Returning coaches enter their email and password. A saved session opens Pep Talk directly. New coaches use Request an account, choose their actual team and role, and wait for approval. The approval email leads to password setup.

3

Request team approval

A first-time coach chooses their team and actual role, provides contact information and submits the request. The approval email identifies the account to set up. Returning coaches can skip this step.

4

Set a password once

After approval, enter the same email, request the six-digit code, then enter that code and choose a password. If two roles share an email, use the invitation for the intended account.

5

Turn on staff Pep Talk

Once approved, the travel head coach chooses Turn on Pep Talk. Approved assistants on the same team inherit access. An owner pause or a school restriction still applies.

6

Read and reply in Pep Talk

The first tab shows the latest staff messages automatically. The approved travel head coach turns it on once. Approved assistants on that team open the same conversation. Use Open conversation & reply to send a staff message.

7

Connect the season workbook

In Events & uploads, open Team season spreadsheet. The lead coach imports the workbook into Google Sheets, grants editors access, and saves its link for this season. Staff use that same link for later edits.

8

Invite a trusted score assistant

In the Coach Portal, choose Manage Score Assistants. Enter the reporter’s name and email, enable trusted reporting, acknowledge the responsibility and send the invitation.

9

Report the final

The coach invites a score assistant and marks them trusted. The assistant accepts the score-only invitation, opens the game, enters both totals and submits. A trusted final publishes immediately. Other assistants send scores for coach approval.

10

Find the published score

The coach opens Scores and chooses Upload stats screenshot beside the final. For a manually reported game, choose Add stats to a published score.

11

Check the screenshot and publish

Crop the MaxPreps stats table so the headings and rows are readable. Pep fills the draft with recognized text. Check each name and number, correct errors, then choose Publish reviewed stats. The existing final score stays attached.

12

See what families see

Open View published game stats. Only the coach-reviewed details appear alongside the final. The original screenshot stays on the coach’s device.
