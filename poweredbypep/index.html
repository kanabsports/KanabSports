04 A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

What is Powered by Pep? +

Powered by Pep is the umbrella behind focused software products like Kanab Sports and Soft Monday. Each product solves a specific kind of everyday work.

Is Soft Monday available now? +

Soft Monday is preparing for a limited campground beta planned for December 2026. Visit SoftMonday.app to learn more as the beta takes shape.

Is Kanab Sports only for Kanab? +

Kanab is our hometown pilot. The goal is a community-first model that can eventually make local sports easier to follow and manage in other towns, too.
