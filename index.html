Why Kanab Sports?

Our town. Our teams. One place.

Kanab Sports started on the sidelines of girls travel soccer.

At first, it seemed like there wasn’t much interest. But when we talked with other parents, we heard something different: they simply didn’t know it was happening. That sparked an idea—one central place where everyone could find opportunities to play, follow their teams and get involved.

That includes visitors. If you’re in Kanab for a few days, finding a trail, a court or somewhere to get active shouldn’t require advance planning or signing up for a season.

As I built Kanab Sports, another need became clear. Every team deserves a place to share its results and celebrate its wins, including teams whose games aren’t streamed or automatically reported. Coaches needed an easy way to get that information out.

That idea grew into the Coaches Portal we’re developing: a shared space for coaches and assistants to manage schedules, report scores and communicate with players, parents and fellow coaches. A built-in chatbot will help coaches prepare and share updates. Messages will identify the coach who sent them, and a shared history will help the coaching staff see what families have already received.

For parents, the goal is one simple portal connected to their teams’ schedules, rosters, reminders and coach updates.

The gear started with a personal frustration: I couldn’t find a Cowboys shirt that fit well and had a design I’d want to wear beyond game day. So I started designing my own, printed on high-quality Comfort Colors T-shirts.

The store also celebrates Kanab beyond sports, with “Kanab Love” tees, “Better Out East” designs for the east side of Kanab, and neighborhood designs that let you represent your own little corner of town.

Kanab Sports exists to help more people show up—for a game, for their kids, for each other.

Jeff Roth · Founder, Kanab Sports

Chalk Walk

October 26 · 1–6 PM
Kanab Event Center

Take a stroll, enjoy the chalk artwork and vote for your favorites. Public viewing and artist participation are free.

Come enjoy the art

View the finished artwork October 26 from 1–6 PM. Public judging takes place during viewing, with votes submitted by QR code.

Create your own

Register in person October 23, 10 AM–3 PM, at the Kanab Event Center. Create your chalk art October 23–26; artwork must be finished by noon October 26.

Artist details, supplies & age groups
  • All Halloween-themed chalk art is welcome. Artwork must follow this year’s event theme to qualify for judging; ask the organizer for the theme and criteria.
  • Chalk is provided while supplies last. Bring your own soft/chalk pastels for detail work.
  • One audience winner per age category: 7 and under, 8–10, 11–13, 14–17, and adult.
