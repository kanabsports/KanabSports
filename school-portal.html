School contact form

Use “Download this team’s contact form” after signing in for the easiest, prefilled version. The blank download below is a layout sample; it cannot approve students without a matching submitted roster. The coach’s roster starts unverified. After sign-in, choose the team and download its populated form. Fill in each authorized guardian’s email using school records. For a second guardian, copy the student’s filled-in row to an empty entry line and enter the second email; leave the email blank when a contact has not been verified. Pep skips those rows and keeps those students pending. Students without an approved contact receive no access through this import.

Download blank contact form

The form has 40 numbered entry rows and clearly labeled columns. In the team-specific download, Pep fills in student names, IDs, team, season and end date. The school mainly fills in parent or guardian emails. Leave unused rows blank. Include no birth dates, home addresses, medical information or custody documents.

School approval enables app messages and eligible push notifications. This form does not enroll anyone in SMS. Original files are not uploaded; confirmed contact records and approval history remain in Pep. Clear temporary downloaded copies according to school policy.

1. Review the coach’s roster

Are these returning students?

Pep found previous school records with matching names. Confirm they are the same students before reusing their IDs. Two students with the same name must keep separate IDs.

Pep Student ID is an internal matching code, not a government ID or an access password. Pep creates it automatically. Keep the filled-in ID, team code, season and end date unchanged. Contact the coach if the student roster needs correcting. Expiration is at the start of the listed date in UTC.

2. Reuse verified contacts or upload a completed form

Another sport this school year?

Match by the same school, school year, student reference and name. Select eligible students below, then review their prior verified contacts. Approval adds the new team to My Family and creates a private in-app notice with an opt-out for this sport. This is school verification, not proof of parent consent.

Revoked contacts and prior opt-outs are excluded. Students without a match need a completed contact form.
