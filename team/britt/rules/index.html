Grades 3–5 Soccer Rules

← Back to team page

Game day basics

9v9 including goalkeeper. Size 4 ball. Four 10-minute quarters, 2-minute breaks after quarters 1 and 3, and a 3-minute halftime. Every player must play at least 50% of the game.

Substitutions

No limit on substitutions with permission. Substitutions are allowed before your team’s throw-in or corner kick; before either team’s goal kick; after a goal; after an injury stoppage; and at halftime.

Equipment & sideline

League jersey required. Tennis shoes or cleats allowed; no toe cleats or metal cleats. Remove jewelry, watches, and dangerous items. Coaches and spectators stay behind spectator lines and never behind goals.

Offside

No offside is called for grades 3–5, though coaches are asked to begin teaching the concept.

Restarts

Kickoff

Players begin in their own half and opponents stay outside the center circle until the kick. Wait for the official’s signal. The ball may be kicked forward or backward.

Throw-in

Use both hands and throw from behind and over the head with both feet grounded. Opponents stay at least 2 yards away. An improper throw gives possession to the other team.

Corner & goal kicks

On corner kicks, opponents stay 10 yards from the corner arc. Goal kicks may be taken anywhere in the goal box; opponents stay outside the penalty box until the ball is kicked and clearly moving.

Free kicks

Direct kicks may score without another touch. Indirect kicks require another player to touch the ball before a goal counts. Opponents stay 10 yards away.

Fouls & goalkeepers

No kicking, tripping, pushing, holding, striking, dangerous charging, handball, or slide tackles. Dangerous play and impeding are indirect-free-kick offenses.

Penalty kicks

For grades 3–5, take the kick from 10 yards from the goal line. Only the kicker and keeper are inside the penalty area.

Goalkeeper essentials

Do not contact a keeper controlling the ball. The handbook sets a 6-second control limit. A keeper cannot use hands on a ball directly kicked or thrown in by a teammate.
