School records belong under school control

Required before school conversations launch: school-controlled access, conversation logs, retention and recovery under the school’s communication policies. Pep’s owner and support staff must not be able to read private conversations or identifying school records.

The planned connection is set up by the school administrator. Storage or encryption keys must remain under school control, with no Pep master key. Support receives only technical status and a random support reference, never personal details, messages or attachments.

This privacy boundary is a launch requirement, not a claim about the current system. Implementation and verification are still required.

The school communication standardSchool-controlled communication. No personal-number texting. Every message logged. District communication policies enforced by Pep.

Each school rollout is subject to district approval, an agreed communication policy and verification of the required safeguards before launch.

Students receive updates

Practice changes, game details and team announcements arrive through the approved school channel. No private coach–student chat in the initial pilot.

The pilot scope includes announcement-only student access across approved channels, with SMS opt-out available.

Parents can communicate

Verified guardians can ask the coach a question through a school-approved, recorded thread. Families receive a clear alternative contact route for urgent concerns.

Guardian access follows district authorization, custody restrictions and applicable student-record rights.

The school owns oversight

The district approves staff, assigns access and sets the communication policy. Authorized administrators retain access to required records when a coach leaves.

District policies govern professional boundaries, student privacy and record retention.

How responsibilities are accounted for

Pep's implementation responsibilities

  • Enforce district-approved access and communication settings
  • Record actual senders, recipients, content, attachments and changes
  • Protect school data and prevent cross-organization access
  • Support agreed exports, retention, legal holds and deletion
  • Document security, providers and incident notification procedures

The district's responsibilities

  • Approve the platform, users, policies and authorized purposes
  • Verify staff roles, guardian relationships and publication permissions
  • Set retention schedules and appoint records and safety contacts
  • Train staff and supervise appropriate use
  • Handle investigations, records requests and required external reporting
Clear responsibilities. The school agreement defines service responsibilities, incident handling and remedies for district review. Schools retain their duties to supervise staff and report suspected abuse to the appropriate authorities. Pep supports these processes; it does not eliminate liability or replace emergency services.

Before launch: the district review packet

Service agreement and Utah-appropriate DPA; data inventory and purposes; subprocessor list; security and incident-response documentation; communication-policy mapping; approved retention/export process; test results and named district owners. USBE templates and vendor guidance.

What your district verifies before launch

The acceptance review covers student reply restrictions, school and family access boundaries, staff removal, message delivery, record exports, retention holds and recovery. Pep and the district review the results before real student data is introduced.

Planned school-only conversation access

Parents will choose from approved coaches. Only school-authorized participants and personnel may access private conversations and identifying audit records. Pep’s owner must have no readable access through databases, logs, backups, support tools or recovery. School-controlled storage or encryption keys and a simple school-admin connection flow must be implemented and verified before launch. Escalating a support issue must not disclose personal details or unlock school records.

Not live yet. Current Pep storage does not meet this owner-inaccessible requirement.

Public community pages and private school data

The school rollout requires private contacts and messages to remain separate from public scores and sponsorships, with no use of school records for sponsor targeting or unauthorized AI training. Public names, rosters and photos require school authorization and the applicable consent or directory-information process.
