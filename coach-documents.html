Schedule: Date, Opponent (or Event), Time, Home/Away, Location. Use MM/DD/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD dates.

One row per player or event, up to 100 rows. Keep the column headings when saving your CSV.

School teams: use School Roster for school approval. Travel / rec roster uploads do not grant parent access or messaging permission.
