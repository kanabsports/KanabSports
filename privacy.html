Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Kanab Sports is a local sports information hub. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Kanab Sports, including team text notifications.

Information we collect

For team communications, we may collect a parent or guardian name, mobile phone number, player name, team association, messaging consent status and timestamps, and records needed to deliver and administer team notifications.

How we use information

We use this information to provide Kanab Sports services and send requested team communications such as practice reminders, game times and locations, schedule changes, cancellations, equipment or uniform reminders, and other team announcements.

SMS privacy

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent data will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers as necessary to operate the messaging service and deliver requested communications.

Kanab Sports team texts are recurring operational messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help. Opting out of SMS does not prevent you from using publicly available Kanab Sports information.

Data protection

Kanab Sports uses reasonable measures to protect information used to operate its services. Information is retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here and applicable operational or legal requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the Kanab Sports website.
