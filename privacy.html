Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Kanab Sports is a local sports information and team communication service. We try to collect only the information needed to make the service work.

Parent Portal

A free Parent Portal account requires a parent or guardian name and email address. Parents may save team associations to their account. Child names are not required. Parents may optionally add a nickname, initials, or another simple family label to help distinguish saved teams.

Public team pages can be viewed without creating a Parent Portal account.

Team communications

For team operations and requested communications, Kanab Sports may process a parent or guardian name, mobile phone number, team association, messaging consent status and timestamps, and team or roster information supplied by an authorized coach or organization when needed to operate that team.

Parent Portal accounts and SMS enrollment are separate. Creating a Parent Portal account does not enroll anyone in text messages.

How we use information

We use information to provide saved team views, schedules, coach updates, account access, and requested team communications such as practice reminders, game times and locations, schedule changes, cancellations, equipment or uniform reminders, and other team announcements.

SMS privacy

Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent data will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We may use service providers as necessary to operate the messaging service and deliver requested communications.

Kanab Sports team texts are recurring operational messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Your choices

You can use public Kanab Sports information without a Parent Portal account. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help.

Data protection

Kanab Sports uses reasonable measures to protect information used to operate its services and aims to retain only what is reasonably needed for those services and applicable operational requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the Kanab Sports website.
